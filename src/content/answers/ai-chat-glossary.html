The words behind long AI chats, defined

Short answer: most long-chat problems come down to two limits: the context window, which is how much the model can read at once, and the browser page, which is how much it can draw. The terms below explain both, plus the limits and tools around them.

The same few words come up whenever a conversation gets long: context, tokens, memory, limits. They are easy to mix up, and mixing them up leads to the wrong fix, such as buying a bigger plan for a problem in the browser tab. Each definition here is short enough to quote.
