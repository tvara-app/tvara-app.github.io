A long chat extension for Grok

Tvara is a Chrome extension for long Grok conversations. It keeps a long chat responsive, lets you jump to any message, shows the queries Grok says you have left, and keeps your Grok history on your own computer.

What Tvara adds to Grok

  • Speed engine. Messages outside the screen are put to sleep and wake as you scroll back to them. Nothing is deleted.
  • Minimap and outline. One mark per message on the right edge, and a table of contents built from your prompts and the headings in the answers.
  • In-chat search. Search across the whole conversation, including messages that are asleep.
  • Queries remaining. The figure Grok itself reports, when it reports one, and "not reported" when it does not.

On Grok every one of these is free.

Your Grok history, kept on your computer

Tvara copies your Grok conversations into a local archive in the background, and the same chat is found whichever link you open it by. Exporting the archive is free. Searching every archived chat at once, encrypted backups and Context Bridge are part of Pro: $1 once, on up to five devices.

Private by design

No chat text, title or prompt is sent to Tvara. The only server is the licence issuer, and it never sees a conversation.
